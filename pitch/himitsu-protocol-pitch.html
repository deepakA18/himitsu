THE RELAYER’S ROLE

Submit the transaction

The relayer signs an outer transaction and calls the pool.

Pay for the gas

The relayer pays ETH for gas and receives a fee from the withdrawal.

Handle the request

The relayer can refuse or delay submission and can log request metadata.
